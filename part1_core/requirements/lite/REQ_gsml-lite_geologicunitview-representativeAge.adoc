[[req_gsml-lite_geologicunitview-representativeAge]]

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/gsml-lite/geologicunitview-representativeAge
part:: representativeAge_uri value SHALL refer to a controlled concept specifying the most representative stratigraphic age interval for the GeologicUnit. This will be defined entirely at the discretion of the data provider and may be a single event selected from the geologic feature’s geological history or a value summarising the all or part of the feature’s history.
====
