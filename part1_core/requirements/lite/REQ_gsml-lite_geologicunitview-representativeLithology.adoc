[[req_gsml-lite_geologicunitview-representativeLithology]]

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/gsml-lite/geologicunitview-representativeLithology
part:: representativeLithology_uri value SHALL refer to a controlled concept specifying the characteristic or representative lithology of the unit. This may be a concept that defines the super-type of all lithology values present within a GeologicUnit or a concept defining the lithology of the dominant CompositionPart (as defined in GeoSciML) of the unit.
====
